Why the thinking behind this version

When you hire a studio you are really hiring a few people, and buyers of an "AI-native" studio quietly wonder whether a human is doing the work at all. This version answers that with faces. After the hero and a quiet row of client logos (B), a founder says in a short signed note what we believe (C). Then the three ways to work with us (D), each shown through the people on that team rather than a price. The work (E) carries the same faces, so people and results back each other up. Clients then talk about working with us (F), and the close offers a call with a named lead (G). Trade-off: it's the warmest version, but it needs real people willing to be on the page, and it dates every time the team changes.

How to read this board: the big frame on the left is the full homepage, top to bottom. Each section is lettered (A, B, C…) and carries a small dashed tag naming the persuasion principle it relies on. Those principles are explained in plain words on the round 1 Overview board. The right column shows the phone version, a section-by-section summary and open questions.
