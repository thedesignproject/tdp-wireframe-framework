Why the thinking behind this version

People buy from people. When you hire a studio you are really hiring the handful of humans who will sit in your Slack every day, so this version shows them. After the same hero as every version, a founder talks to camera for 60 seconds (B), then you meet the team by face, role and specialty (C). Next is a single row showing who on your side talks to whom on ours (D), so it's clear you get named people, not a ticket queue. Two clients then say the people were easy to work with (E), we show the two studios in Miami and Buenos Aires (F), and the close asks you to book with a named founder, not a sales form (G). Trade-off: it's the warmest version and the hardest to make: it needs real photos, a founder willing to be on camera, and an update every time the team changes.

How to read this board: the big frame on the left is the full homepage, top to bottom. Each section is lettered (A, B, C…) and carries a small dashed tag naming the persuasion principle it relies on. Those principles are explained in plain words on the Overview board. The right column shows the phone version, a section-by-section summary and open questions.
