Why the thinking behind this version

Every buyer arrives with the same few doubts about hiring an outside studio. Instead of an FAQ, this version uses those doubts as the page's headings and answers each one with real work. After the hero and a row of client logos (B), four doubts follow, each answered by a project: speed by Imera (C), quality with AI by Socure (D), fitting your team by Monte Carlo (E), and risk by our guarantee and what clients say (F). With the doubts gone, the three ways to work appear with full prices (G), and the page asks for the call (H). Trade-off: naming a doubt can plant one the visitor didn't have, so every heading has to be answered right away and with proof.

How to read this board: the big frame on the left is the full homepage, top to bottom. Each section is lettered (A, B, C…) and carries a small dashed tag naming the persuasion principle it relies on. Those principles are explained in plain words on the round 1 Overview board. The right column shows the phone version, a section-by-section summary and open questions.
