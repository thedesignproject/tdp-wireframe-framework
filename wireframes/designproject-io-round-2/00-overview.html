Why the thinking behind round 2

Round 1 drew the five stories raised on the 2 Nov call. Round 2 tries five stories nobody has proposed yet, so the team picks from a wider field instead of the first ideas in the room. The rules are unchanged: the hero is frozen, the process stays off the homepage, and case studies are never the first proof. Each new version bets on a principle that round 1 used only as support: seeing the work (V6), knowing the price (V7), meeting the people (V8), answering the doubts (V9) and proving speed (V10). Read them next to round 1. The best homepage is likely one spine from either round plus one or two borrowed sections.

How to read this board: the five columns are the five new versions, read top to bottom like a scroll. The first step (Hero) is solid because it's the same everywhere; the dashed steps are what changes. The persuasion principles are explained in plain words on the round 1 Overview board.
