Why the thinking behind this version

A stranger decides in a few seconds whether to keep scrolling. So this version puts the proof in the first screen, next to the headline: a real project, with the client saying in their own words what it did for them (A). The words are the same as in every other version; only the right half of the hero is new. Below it, client logos (B) show it wasn't one client, then our results (C), where every project card leads with its number. Only then do we say what we do (D), show that we build in public (E), and ask for the call (F). Trade-off: the hero gets busier, and the headline has to share the first screen with a picture.

How to read this board: the big frame on the left is the full homepage, top to bottom. Each section is lettered (A, B, C…) and carries a small dashed tag naming the persuasion principle it relies on. Those principles are explained in plain words on the round 1 Overview board. The right column shows the phone version, a section-by-section summary and open questions.
