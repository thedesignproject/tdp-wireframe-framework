designproject.io · homepage wireframes · round 1

Same hero. Five ways to tell the story after it.

Positioning is agreed: a design-led, AI-native product studio. The hero is identical in every version, so we compare the flow, not the headline. Each version tests one hypothesis from the 2 Nov call.

How to review: read each spine top to bottom, out loud, as a stranger who has never heard of TDP. Pick the one where you'd book a call soonest. Then open its board for the full page, mobile and notes.
