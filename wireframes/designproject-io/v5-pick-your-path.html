Why the thinking behind this version

The call agreed we need industry pages (HealthTech first). This version turns the homepage into a signpost that sends each visitor to the right one. Visitors pick their industry and see results from companies like theirs (B). Then they pick the sentence that sounds like them, which shows the matching plan and price (C). A free 5-minute assessment (D) gives them something useful before we ask for anything. Each small choice ("this is us") makes booking a call (F) feel like the natural next step rather than a leap. Trade-off: it's a signpost rather than a story, and it only works once each industry page exists with at least two results.

How to read this board: the big frame on the left is the full homepage, top to bottom. Each section is lettered (A, B, C…) and carries a small dashed tag naming the persuasion principle it relies on. Those principles are explained in plain words on the Overview board. The right column shows the phone version, a section-by-section summary and open questions.
