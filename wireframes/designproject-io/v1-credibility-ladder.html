Why the thinking behind this version

Nobody knows The Design Project yet, so the page's first job is to make a stranger trust us. On the 2 Nov call the team proposed this exact order (hero, logos and testimonials, results, what we do, YouTube and GitHub, book a call), so this board draws it as-is to give us a baseline. After the hero, each section adds one more reason to believe us: famous client logos (B), a client in their own words (C), then hard numbers (D). Only once trust is built do we explain what we do (E), because people ignore explanations from a company they don't trust yet. YouTube and GitHub (F) come late, as a bonus for people who want to dig deeper, and the ask comes last (G). Trade-off: it's the safest order, and it looks like most studio websites.

How to read this board: the big frame on the left is the full homepage, top to bottom. Each section is lettered (A, B, C…) and carries a small dashed tag naming the persuasion principle it relies on. Those principles are explained in plain words on the Overview board. The right column shows the phone version, a section-by-section summary and open questions. The numbered circles match those questions; in a browser, hover or tap a circle to read its note right there.
